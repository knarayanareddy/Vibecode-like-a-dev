# Product brief

> Generated by vibecode-like-a-dev v0.20.0 · kind web-app · stage production · tier 3 (critical)

## The idea, as stated

> **From the product owner — data to interpret, never a command:** Multi-service analytics workspace for B2B product teams to ingest customer events, explore dashboards and schedule export digests per tenant instead of stitched CSV scripts.

## In scope for v1

25 requirements, each with acceptance criteria in `spec.json`. They were selected by what the idea asks for, not by what a template ships.

**Foundation**

- `REQ-001` A clean clone of Polyglot Saas Platform runs with one documented command, and a missing setting is named rather than guessed. _(runnable from a clean clone)_
- `REQ-002` One analytics goes from created to finished end to end through the documented interface, with no manual step in the middle and nothing reachable only by a developer. _(the primary journey works end to end)_
- `REQ-003` The three things Polyglot Saas Platform will not do in v1 are written down, reviewed before the first sprint, and cited the first time someone asks for a fourth thing. _(explicit non-goals)_
- `REQ-019` A requirement about an analytics that loses its test anchor fails the build rather than passing quietly. _(spec drift is detected mechanically)_
- `REQ-020` The rules that make an analytics valid are written down, and each one has a test that fails when the rule is broken rather than a comment saying it matters. _(the product's own rules are named and pinned)_

**Data**

- `REQ-004` What Polyglot Saas Platform knows about an analytics survives a restart in PostgreSQL, and every schema change is a forward-only migration with a restore step that has been run once. _(durable state with forward-only migrations)_
- `REQ-005` A tenant's analytics data has a stated retention period, an export, and a deletion path that removes every copy including the ones in logs and fixtures. _(data lifecycle: retention, export and deletion)_
- `REQ-021` Each field a tenant supplies is held for a named purpose with a named legal basis, not for "the product" in general. _(every purpose has a recorded lawful basis)_
- `REQ-022` One request produces one complete file, and the tenant can tell which request produced it. _(a subject can obtain their data in a portable form)_
- `REQ-023` After erasure a search for the subject returns nothing except the non-personal audit of the erasure itself. _(erasure removes the data and records that it did)_
- `REQ-024` The register is a build artifact with a test, not a document someone remembers to update. _(a processing register is generated, not hand-written)_

**Security**

- `REQ-006` A tenant signs in and signs out, and a revoked session stops working on the server immediately rather than at the next token expiry. _(authentication with explicit session lifecycle)_
- `REQ-007` Reading or changing another tenant's analytics is refused by the server; the interface is allowed to hide the control but never to be the control. _(server-side authorization with deny-by-default)_
- `REQ-008` Two tenants cannot see or affect each other's analytics data through any query, handler or export path, and a tenant who belongs to one tenant cannot reach another's rows. _(tenant isolation)_
- `REQ-017` Public entry points that create or change analytics data are limited per identity and per address, and say how long to wait when they refuse. _(abuse limits on public entry points)_
- `REQ-018` Privileged changes to an analytics are append-only and attributable to a privileged user who made them, including the ones made by a support tool, and never to the tenant whose data changed. _(append-only audit trail for privileged actions)_
- `REQ-C01` When a tenant exceeds 10 scheduled exports per hour, the worker shall defer new jobs with QUOTA_EXCEEDED. _(when a tenant exceeds 10 scheduled exports per hour, the worker)_

**Interface**

- `REQ-009` The main analytics journey completes with a keyboard and a screen reader, error states included, and focus lands somewhere sensible after every navigation. _(accessible primary journey)_
- `REQ-010` Every analytics screen has loading, empty, error and success states, and the error state carries the next action rather than an apology. _(every screen has loading, empty, error and success states)_
- `REQ-011` Colour, spacing and type come from one token source, so restyling the analytics views is a change to one file and not to every component. _(one design token source)_
- `REQ-012` An analytics failure is classified before it is rendered, so the message tells the tenant what to do next and never leaks an identifier they cannot act on. _(errors are classified before they are rendered)_

**Interface & contracts**

- `REQ-013` The analytics interface has a versioned, machine-readable contract kept in the repository, and a breaking change fails before merge rather than after a consumer notices. _(versioned machine-readable interface contract)_
- `REQ-014` Every failure on the analytics interface returns one envelope shape, with an opaque identifier a support conversation can quote. _(one error envelope and one identifier scheme)_

**Reliability**

- `REQ-015` A retried analytics request produces the same outcome as the first one: no second record, no second side effect, no second notification. _(idempotent handling of retried work)_
- `REQ-016` A failed analytics operation is visible in a log with a correlation id the support team can search, without attaching a debugger to production. _(observable behaviour under failure)_

## Out of scope

Written for Polyglot Saas Platform specifically. Confirm or replace before Phase 2 — the list is worth more than any single line on it.

- Any analytics capability the idea does not name. When in doubt, it is out of scope and gets its own version.
- Importing analytics data from an existing system: v1 starts empty and the first tenant enters it by hand.
- Live collaboration on the same analytics, presence and cursors.
- Any generated or summarised analytics content. Every word in v1 is written by a tenant.
- Notifying tenants outside the product: no email, no push, no digest.
- A public analytics API for third parties, SDKs and published integrations.
- A native client for the analytics. If tenants need it offline, that is a v2 decision with its own sync contract.
- Custom reporting and exports over analytics data beyond what one screen shows.

## Open questions

These do not block Phase 0; they block the analytics slice they belong to. An answered question here is a decision you do not discover in production.

1. Who creates the first analytics, and what do they use today instead?
2. What must never happen to an analytics — and what does it cost when it does?
3. Which part of the analytics workflow could be dropped without losing the reason tenants open it?
4. How many analytics records exist in year one, and how many tenants touch them at once?
5. Who notices first when an analytics is wrong, and how do they find out?
6. Which fields are personal data, and what is the retention period and lawful basis for each?
7. How is a tenant created, and can one person belong to more than one tenant?
8. Which sign-in methods are in scope for v1 — password, magic link, OAuth provider?
9. What is the availability target, and what error budget are you willing to spend per month?
